The App has no sign-up and no login, and is built to work with as little personal data as possible.

1. What we never collect

The App does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

2. Data kept on your device

Quiz progress, level, Flag Points, favourites, collected cards, mini-game records, the nickname you chose for the rankings, settings and whether you own Pro are stored on your device. The App also keeps a short log of in-game events on your device — at most 200, such as “levelled up” — to help diagnose problems; it is never sent anywhere.

Settings → Reset Progress erases your quiz progress, level, achievements, favourites, collected cards, review queue and best scores. It deliberately keeps your Flag Points, your Pro purchase and your settings: points are earned by watching ads, and a purchase is not progress.

On iOS, when «iCloud Sync» is on (Settings; on by default), a copy of this record is also kept in your own iCloud account so it carries over to your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. The developer cannot read or delete that copy — it lives inside your account. Android has no such feature.

3. Rankings (optional)

When the App starts, and when it returns to the foreground, it makes one small request to the game service (Firebase) to see whether the network can be reached. That request carries no account, no identifier and nothing about you; as with any internet request, the receiving server does see your IP address.

Your ranking record is created only when you open the rankings and choose a nickname. From then on the App sends to Firebase: an anonymous player ID created for this app (not your Apple ID), the nickname you chose, a country flag derived from your device’s region setting, the display colour and badge you chose for your row (cosmetic only), and your scores. When you finish a ranked round it also sends that round’s answers, so that the server rather than your device decides the score; the answers are graded and discarded, and are not stored. That is the entire record. To have an entry deleted, write to the address in section 10.

How the game service handles data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Anthem playback (optional)

When you tap play on a national anthem, the recording is streamed from the Internet Archive (archive.org), the public library that hosts the public-domain U.S. Navy Band collection the App uses. That service receives your IP address and the name of the file requested. Nothing else is sent, and nothing at all is sent if you never tap play.

How the Internet Archive handles data: https://archive.org/about/terms.php

5. Ads

The free version shows a rewarded ad only when you tap “Watch an ad” to earn Flag Points. There are no banner ads and no full-screen ads. Ads are served by AdMob, which may use an advertising identifier to select and measure them.

  • On iOS the App asks your permission before any tracking. If you decline, ads are non-personalised.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK) a consent form asks how your data may be used, and your choice is applied.
  • Pro users are shown no ads at all.

How the ad provider handles this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Purchases

Purchases are processed by App Store. The developer never sees or stores your card number or billing address. The App keeps only a yes/no flag for whether Pro is owned, and checks it against the store.

7. Notifications (optional)

The daily flag reminder is an iPhone and iPad feature; the Android version does not have it. If you turn it on, the App asks for notification permission and then schedules the reminder on your device. Nothing is sent to a server and no push service is involved. You can turn it off at any time.

8. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It has no chat, no messaging, and no user-supplied content other than a nickname you choose yourself.

9. Retention and deletion

On-device data is kept until you reset it or uninstall the App. Ranking entries live as long as their board: daily boards roll over each day, weekly boards each week. Uninstalling removes the data on the device. If iCloud Sync was on, a copy stays in your own iCloud account and comes back when you reinstall with the same Apple ID; turning the setting off stops any further upload.

10. Contact and changes

Questions, or a request to delete a ranking entry: ddontti_studio@outlook.kr

This policy may change; changes are published with app updates.

5. Reklamy

Wersja bezpłatna wyświetla reklamę z nagrodą tylko wtedy, gdy dotkniesz „Obejrzyj reklamę”, aby zdobyć Punkty Flagowe. Nie ma reklam banerowych ani pełnoekranowych. Reklamy dostarcza AdMob, który może używać identyfikatora reklamowego do ich doboru i pomiaru.

  • W systemie iOS Aplikacja prosi o Twoją zgodę przed jakimkolwiek śledzeniem. Jeśli odmówisz, reklamy nie będą spersonalizowane.

Sposób przetwarzania tych danych przez dostawcę reklam: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
